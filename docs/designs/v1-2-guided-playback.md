# Design: Atlas Trails v1.2 — guided playback, safer viewer, clean domain move

Generated by /office-hours on 2026-10-31
Branch: main
Repo: ShreyRavi/atlas-trails
Status: APPROVED
Mode: Builder

## Problem Statement
A shared Atlas Trails link draws a trip across a 3D globe, but it flies past every
stop without stopping. The person who made the trip wants viewers to *follow* it:
"when I zoom into location x, I want to read what they did and how long they stayed
there." Alongside that, the live app has a confirmed XSS hole, the read-only viewer
can't pass a link on, destructive actions happen with no confirmation, any crash
shows a misleading "needs WebGL" screen, and the app is moving to
atlas.kansoboard.com.

## What Makes This Cool
The share link stops being a map and becomes a short documentary of a trip. The
camera lands, the stop card fades in ("Porto · Mar 6 – 9 · **3 nights**", plus what
happened), it holds long enough to read, then flies on. The pacing *is* the story.

## Constraints
- Stays backendless: the whole trip lives in the URL hash (`#t=`, lz-string). Card
  content stays short (140-char notes, one link per stop).
- Static Vite build on Vercel, deployed by hand with the CLI (not git-integrated).
- No test suite today (only `oxlint` and `tsc -b`).
- Dates only, no times; stay length is shown in nights.

## Premises
1. The heart of v1.2 is the viewer experience: a shared link plays like a story.
   Builder-side changes support that. *(agreed)*
2. Stay backendless, with content kept short in the URL. *(agreed)*
3. The XSS fix ships on its own, first, as a hotfix deploy. *(agreed)*
4. Extend existing pieces (`usePlayback` flow, `formatDates`, `tripStats`,
   `cleanText`/`cleanDate`, `ErrorBoundary`) rather than replace them. *(agreed)*
5. **Revised (D5):** v1.2 is a clean new version on atlas.kansoboard.com. No
   compatibility work is owed to the old domain, and old-domain localStorage
   (autosave and library) is not migrated. **Decision:** in Vercel domain settings,
   `atlas-trails.vercel.app` 308-redirects to `https://atlas.kansoboard.com`.
   Browsers carry the `#t=` fragment across the redirect, so old links happen to
   keep opening (no extra code), and nobody can keep minting old-domain share
   links. Data saved on the old origin is stranded, which is accepted.

## Cross-Model Perspective
Independent cold read (Claude subagent; Codex not installed):
- **Coolest unconsidered version:** play on the trip's own calendar, with a date
  odometer and a day/night terminator that follows the sun for each date, so a
  3-night stay shows as three light sweeps over the pin.
- **Key insight:** the quote above shows the user cares about pacing as storytelling.
- **50% prior art:** mapbox/storytelling (chapter → flyTo → caption → advance).
  Take the UX pattern, not the code.
- **Weekend build:** a stop-indexed playback state machine. It flagged that today's
  `step` indexes `arcs`, and `tripArcs` drops consecutive duplicate stops, so a card
  keyed on `step` would show the wrong stop's note.
- **Challenged premise #5:** localStorage is per-origin, so a domain move strands
  saved trips. The user chose to accept that (see revised premise 5).
- Also flagged: the per-stop link needs a strict http(s) check, and `name`/`country`
  have no length cap.

## Approaches Considered
### Approach A: Extend in place
Grow the existing `usePlayback` timer effects. Smallest diff, but timing logic stays
spread across effects and is hard to test. Not chosen.
### Approach B: Pure playback state machine — CHOSEN
A pure, stop-indexed reducer plus a thin hook that only runs timers and the camera.
### Approach C: Timeline-first
B's engine with a nights-proportional timeline as the main control. Deferred: it can
sit on top of B later without rework.

## Recommended Approach
**Approach B**, shipped as two PRs:

**PR 1, hotfix (deploy immediately):** HTML-escape `pointLabel` in
`GlobeCanvas.tsx`. Confirmed sink: float-tooltip calls `tooltipEl.html(content)`.
Cap `name`/`country` at 80 chars in `parseStops` via `cleanText` (D15).

**PR 2, v1.2:**
- **Playback engine:** a pure reducer over *stop indices*. State is
  `{ phase: idle|flying|dwelling|paused|done, index, remainingMs, resumeTo }`.
  It is used by **both** the viewer's and the builder's Play: the builder's Play
  previews exactly what viewers will see. One engine, no second mode.
  - **Every transition is timer-driven, never camera-event-driven.** The hook calls
    `pointOfView(target, FLY_MS)` and sets a timer for `FLY_MS`. A hop to the same
    coordinates (a consecutive duplicate stop) uses a fixed 300 ms transition, so
    playback can never stall waiting on a camera that doesn't move.
  - **Transition table:**

    | phase \ event | tick (timer fired) | pause | resume | next | prev | jump(i) | userDrag | stop/edit |
    |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
    | idle | — | — | — | — | — | — | — | — |
    | flying | → dwelling (remainingMs = dwellMs) | → paused (resumeTo=flying) | — | index+1, → flying | index−1 (min 0), → flying | index=i, → flying | → paused (resumeTo=flying) | → idle |
    | dwelling | last stop ? → done : index+1, → flying | → paused (resumeTo=dwelling, keep remainingMs) | — | same as tick | index−1 (min 0), → flying | index=i, → flying | → paused (resumeTo=dwelling) | → idle |
    | paused | — | — | → flying (always re-fly to `index`, then dwell for remainingMs, or the full dwell if paused mid-flight) | index+1, → flying | index−1, → flying | index=i, → flying | — | → idle |
    | done | — | — | — | — | — | index=i, → flying | — | → idle |

    `play` from idle/done sets index=0 and enters flying; on an empty trip `play`
    is a no-op (stays idle).
  - **Rules that override the table cells:**
    1. **Index is always clamped to 0..N−1.** `next` at the last stop → `done`
       from any phase. `prev` uses min 0 in every row. `jump(i)` with i outside
       0..N−1 is ignored (no state change).
    2. **Arrival (flying/tick):** if `resumeTo === 'dwelling'` (re-flying after
       a pause taken mid-dwell), keep `remainingMs`; otherwise set
       `remainingMs = dwellMs(stops[index])`. Then clear `resumeTo`.
    3. **next/prev/jump from any phase** clear `resumeTo` and reset
       `remainingMs` to the new stop's full dwell, so a jump never inherits the
       previous stop's leftover time.
    4. Pause mid-flight sets `resumeTo='flying'`; on resume and arrival, rule 2
       gives the full dwell.
  - **Stale timers:** each transition increments a `generation` counter. Timers
    capture it and are ignored if it has changed. Calling `pointOfView` again
    supersedes any in-flight camera transition.
  - **User interaction:** any pointer-down/wheel on the globe during
    flying/dwelling dispatches `userDrag` (auto-pause). Resume re-flies to the
    current stop, so the card always matches the pin on screen. autoRotate is off
    from `play` until `done`/`idle`, then back on.
  - **Arcs:** `arcsRevealed(i)` = the number of non-duplicate transitions among
    `stops[0..i]` (pure helper; equivalent to `tripArcs(stops.slice(0, i+1)).length`).
    Arcs beyond the current stop are hidden, so a backward jump hides them. The
    arc for the hop currently flying animates; earlier ones are solid.
  - **Helpers:** `dwellMs(stop) = clamp(2500 + noteLength·K, 2500, 7000)`. It counts
    the note only, and K (30–60 ms) is set by The Assignment. `nights(stop)` follows
    the rules below.
  - **Nights and dates rules** (one helper, used by the StopCard and the builder
    and viewer lists): no `start` → show no dates and no badge; `start` only, or
    `end === start` → "Day trip"; `end > start` → `N night(s)`, where
    N = days between them; `end < start` → treated as start-only ("Day trip").
    `parseStops` already drops such an end on every load/decode (`types.ts`), but
    in-memory builder state can still hold one (a typed date bypasses
    `min={start}`), so the helper handles it rather than trusting the input.
- **StopCard** (bottom-left; never covers the centered pin): stop n/N, name,
  country, dates, nights badge ("Day trip" for a single date), note, optional link.
- **PlaybackBar** (bottom-center): Prev / Pause / Next, a draining dwell-progress
  line, and clickable stop names. Keys: Space, ←, →. On screens under 560px the card
  stacks above the bar.
- **Per-stop link:** new optional `url`, accepted only when `new URL(x).protocol` is
  `http:`/`https:`, capped length, rendered with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`. Carried
  through storage, the library and the share wire format.
- **Nights** also shown in the builder and viewer stop lists.
- **Viewer Share button** re-encodes the trip, rather than copying `location.href`.
- **Modals** on native `<dialog>` (Esc, focus and backdrop built in). Replace the
  `window.prompt` clipboard fallback. Confirm before Reset, Delete saved trip, and
  Load-over-unsaved-work.
  - **"Unsaved work"** is derived on demand, not stored as a flag. It is true when
    the working trip has stops AND `normalize(current)` equals `normalize(entry)`
    for **no** library entry. `normalize(trip)` runs both sides through the same
    sanitizers the library applies on read:
    `{ title: cleanText(title, 80) ?? '', summary: cleanText(summary, SUMMARY_MAX) ?? '', stops: parseStops(stops.map(serializeStop)) }`,
    compared with `JSON.stringify`. Untrimmed notes and in-memory `end < start` are
    then normalized identically on both sides. Content matching across all entries (not
    `currentId`) means a reload, which resets the non-persisted `currentId`, can't
    trigger a false "unsaved" prompt. Computed in `App.tsx`, where both the working
    trip and `library` already live. (`currentId` is also persisted in the
    `atlas.trip.v1` autosave payload, so Save still updates the same library entry
    after a reload. An unknown or missing id in that payload falls back to null.)
- **Error screen:** `Unsupported` shows only when the `hasWebGL()` pre-check fails
  before mounting. Any error caught by a boundary shows the crash screen: the
  app-level boundary shows a full-screen version, and a nested boundary around the
  lazy globe shows a globe-only fallback while the command bar stays usable. The
  crash screen offers Reload, version, and **Start fresh**, which clears the URL
  hash and the autosave key (`atlas.trip.v1`) only. The saved-trip library is never
  touched, so no confirm is needed.
- **Version tag:** `v1.2.0 · <sha>` in the corner, linking to the GitHub commit.
  `vite.config.ts` gets
  `define: { __GIT_SHA__: JSON.stringify(process.env.GIT_SHA ?? 'dev'), __APP_VERSION__: JSON.stringify(pkg.version) }`
  (a plain `GIT_SHA` is not exposed to client code without this). The deploy passes
  `--build-env GIT_SHA=$(git rev-parse --short HEAD)` (flag verified in
  `vercel deploy --help`). With `dev`, the tag shows no link.
- **Domain:** update `og:url`, `og:image` and `twitter:image` in `index.html`, plus
  README links, to atlas.kansoboard.com.
- **Open-editor bug:** track the ✎ editor by a stable client-side stop id, not by
  list position.

### Design review decisions
**Screen hierarchy per phase**
- **Viewer top bar (1.1):** while playing or paused (flying/dwelling/paused),
  the top bar collapses to the trip title plus the one-line `StatsLine`. The
  summary, full stop list and actions are hidden. At idle and done the full bar
  returns (title, summary, stats, stop list, actions). Hierarchy while playing:
  ① StopCard → ② globe + current pin → ③ PlaybackBar → ④ collapsed title strip.
- **Exit (1.2):** the PlaybackBar ends with a **✕ Exit** control in both the
  builder and the viewer, and Esc does the same. Exit dispatches `stop`, giving
  idle with every arc solid and the full top bar (viewer) or the builder UI back.
- **Viewer Share (1.3):** Share goes in the viewer's existing `.cb-actions` row as
  `[▶ Play] [🔗 Share] [Create your own]`, matching the builder. It is hidden while
  playing, along with the rest of the full bar. This overrides the wireframe's
  floating top-right button.

**States**
- **Loading (2.1):** on a shared link, the full top bar (title, summary, stats,
  stop list) renders at once while the globe chunk loads. A small `--panel` chip
  reading "Loading globe…" sits centered below it. The PlaybackBar and the Play
  button stay hidden until `globeReady`; autoplay then starts immediately and the
  bar collapses (1.1).
- **Paused (2.2):** three cues together. The Pause button becomes "▶ Resume" (its
  `aria-label` switches too), the dwell progress line freezes and dims to 40%
  opacity, and a small "Paused" chip appears in the card's top-right corner. All
  three clear on resume.
- **Done (2.3):** after the last card's dwell, the card and bar fade out and the
  camera pulls back to frame every stop. A pure helper `fitAll(stops)` takes the
  centroid of the stop coordinates and derives altitude from their spread, clamped
  to 1.2–2.6. All arcs render solid, then the slow auto-rotate resumes. The full top
  bar returns with stats and the action row: viewer `[↺ Replay] [🔗 Share] [Create
  your own]`; builder shows its normal actions with Play reading "↺ Replay".
- **Sparse and awkward content (2.4):**
  - No note and no dates: the card collapses to `n/N · name · country`, with no
    empty rows and no badge, and the dwell is the 2.5 s minimum. Dates but no note:
    there's no note block, and the link row only appears when a url exists.
  - Single-stop trip: Prev and Next are disabled (`aria-disabled`), and playback
    goes to done after the dwell.
  - Long names (up to the 80-char cap) wrap with `overflow-wrap: anywhere`; never
    truncate.
  - The bar's stop strip scrolls sideways (`overflow-x: auto`, no visible
    scrollbar), and the current stop is scrolled into view on each change. Under
    560px the strip is replaced by a "2 / 5" counter.

  | Feature | Loading | Empty / sparse | Error | Success | Partial |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Shared-link viewer | full top bar + "Loading globe…" chip (2.1) | invalid or empty `#t=` → builder, as today | globe crash → nested boundary fallback; command bar stays | autoplay → cards → done finale (2.3) | stops without notes → collapsed card (2.4) |
  | StopCard | hidden until first arrival | collapsed card (2.4) | — | full card | dates only / note only / no link |
  | PlaybackBar | hidden until `globeReady` | single stop → Prev/Next disabled | — | ⏮ ⏸ ⏭ ✕ + progress | paused → three cues (2.2) |
- **Crash screen and dialogs (2.5):**
  - The crash screen reuses the `Unsupported` layout (centered column, emoji,
    22px heading, one line at 0.7 opacity, 12px gap): "Something went wrong" / "The
    app hit an unexpected error." Reload is the `.cb-btn-primary` (cyan) button;
    Start fresh is `.cb-btn-ghost`, with the helper line "Your saved trips are
    kept." The version tag is shown beneath.
  - Dialogs use native `<dialog>` styled with the `--panel` glass treatment (14px
    radius, 12px blur, `--panel-border`), with a `::backdrop` of
    `rgba(6,9,18,0.6)`. **Cancel gets focus by default.** The destructive action
    is a ghost button with a red label. New token `--danger: #ff6b6b`, used only
    for destructive labels. Copy is in Pass 7.
  - Opening any dialog dispatches `pause` if playback is running.

**Journey: a friend opens a shared link on their phone**

| Step | User does | User feels | Plan specifies |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Taps the link in a chat | Curious: "whose trip?" | Title, stats and stops render at once, plus "Loading globe…" (2.1) |
| 2 | Waits ~1–3 s | Anticipation, not doubt | Chip explains the wait; no blank screen |
| 3 | Globe appears, autoplay starts | "Oh, it moves" | Bar collapses to the title strip (1.1); camera flies to stop 1 |
| 4 | Reads the first card | Drawn in | Card first in the hierarchy; dwell scales with the note |
| 5 | Accidentally touches the globe | Brief "did I break it?" | Auto-pause with three cues; Resume re-flies (2.2) |
| 6 | Taps Next / a stop name | In control | Prev/Next/jump; the card always matches the pin |
| 7 | Last card ends | Satisfied: "that was the whole trip" | Pull back to the full route; arcs solid (2.3) |
| 8 | Sees the actions | "I want one" / "send it on" | Replay · Share · Create your own (1.3, 2.3) |

Time horizons: **5 seconds:** the title and route appear immediately, never a black
screen. **5 minutes:** one full trip plays through with control and no confusion.
**Long term:** the finale frame (the whole route on the globe) is the image people
remember and re-share.

- **Hidden tab (3.1):** `visibilitychange` → `document.hidden` dispatches `pause`
  while flying/dwelling, with the same three paused cues (2.2). Returning does
  **not** auto-resume; the viewer taps Resume and continues exactly where they
  left off.

**Responsive and accessibility**
- **Phone layout (6.1):** under 560px the card is full width (`calc(100vw - 32px)`)
  and stacked directly above the bar, `max-height: 40vh`. The note clamps to 3 lines
  (`-webkit-line-clamp: 3`) with a "more" button; expanding dispatches `pause` and
  shows the full note scrollable within the 40vh cap. **Camera offset:** the hook
  measures the bottom stack's height `H` and the top strip's height `T`, then aims
  `pointOfView` south of the stop by the latitude equal to `(H − T) / 2` screen
  pixels at the current altitude. The pin then sits in the middle of the free band.
  This is a pure helper `centerOffsetLat(H, T, viewportH, altitude)`, tuned on a
  real 375×667 phone. Desktop is unchanged: the card sits bottom-left with the pin
  centered.
- **Hidden during playback (6.2):** the Trips button and panel (builder) and the
  version tag are not rendered while flying/dwelling/paused, and return at
  idle/done. Loading a library trip mid-playback is therefore impossible by
  construction.
- **Touch targets (6.3):** Prev/Pause/Next/Exit are ≥44×44px. Stop names in the bar
  are buttons ≥32px tall with 8px gaps. The existing stop icon buttons (`↑ ↓ × ✎`,
  `.cb-stop-move`/`.cb-stop-x`/`.cb-stop-note-btn`) and `.trips-del` get a ≥44px hit
  area via an absolutely positioned `::before` (`inset: -12px`) without changing
  their visual size. On `(pointer: coarse)`, builder stop pills get `min-height: 36px`.
- **Keyboard and screen reader (6.4):**
  - Shortcuts Space (pause/resume), ← / → (prev/next) and Esc (exit) are active
    only while playing. They are ignored when `event.target` is an
    input/textarea/select/`[contenteditable]` or a `<dialog open>` exists, and
    `preventDefault` is called only when a shortcut is handled. While a dialog is
    open, Esc closes the dialog, not playback.
  - The StopCard is `role="region"` with `aria-label="Current stop"`. A visually
    hidden `aria-live="polite"` node announces "Stop 2 of 5, Porto, Portugal, 3
    nights" on each arrival. Focus never moves to the card.
  - The Pause button's `aria-label` toggles Pause/Resume. Stop-name buttons use
    `aria-current="step"` for the current stop. The progress line and decorative
    icons are `aria-hidden`.
  - `:focus-visible` shows a 2px `--arc` outline with a 2px offset on every
    control. The browser default is removed only where this replaces it.
- **Flights and reduced motion (6.5, user: "I like longer dramatic flights"):**
  - **Default flight is cinematic:** `FLY_MS ≈ 2400` per hop, driven by one
    requestAnimationFrame loop over the pure `flightPath(from, to, t)` (E1/D13):
    great-circle position plus a `sin(πt)` altitude rise peaking at
    `clamp(1.9 + km/4000, 2.2, 3.2)`, with one ease-in-out across the whole
    flight. The camera rides the arc, which draws over the same `FLY_MS`. Short
    hops (<300 km) skip the rise and fly a single 1200 ms segment. Duplicate stops
    keep their 300 ms transition. Phase transitions stay timer-driven (one tick
    timer per flight); the rAF loop only renders the camera, and both are
    cancelled under the same `generation` guard.
  - **`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`:** the camera cuts instantly
    (`pointOfView(…, 0)`), the arc appears whole, the card has no translate (opacity
    only), auto-rotate stays off, and the done finale cuts to the fit-all view. A
    ~400 ms beat replaces the flight, so dwell timing and controls are unchanged.
- **Contrast (6.6):** as part of the restyle, readable text is ≥13px at ≥0.85
  opacity:
  - `.cb-stop-note`: 12px/0.75 → 13px/0.85
  - `.cb-summary-view`: stays at 0.85
  - non-essential labels (`.cb-country`, `.trips-meta`, `.cb-note-count`) ≥12px at
    ≥0.75 (up from 10–12px at 0.45–0.55)
  - placeholders ≥0.55

  Each text and background pair is checked at ≥4.5:1 against `--panel` over the
  globe's brightest region (city lights), and the result is recorded in DESIGN.md.

## Success Criteria
- Opening a shared 6-stop link on a phone: every card shows the right stop (including
  trips with consecutive duplicate stops), is readable before it advances, and
  Pause/Prev/Next never leave playback stuck or desynced from the camera.
- A crafted link with `<img onerror>` in a stop name runs no script on hover.
- A `javascript:` URL in a link field never renders as a clickable link.
- No native `alert`/`confirm`/`prompt` remains in `src/`.
- The corner tag shows the deployed commit SHA on atlas.kansoboard.com.

### R3-1 — completeness (blocking)

**Problem**

> normalize() does not sanitize stops the way the library does, so the comparison can still report a difference when nothing has changed. The note textarea writes raw input into in-memory stops (CommandBar `onEditStop(i, { note: e.target.value })`), so a note can carry leading or trailing whitespace or newlines. An in-memory end can also come before start, which the design itself acknowledges. serializeStop copies note and end as they are. Library entries, however, are re-read through `read()` in trips.ts, which runs parseStops: that trims each note with cleanText and drops any end < start. Summaries are also sliced to SUMMARY_MAX there. After a Save, normalize(current) therefore differs from normalize(entry), and the next Load raises a false 'unsaved work' confirm. That is the same false-prompt failure R2-3 asked to eliminate.

**Remedy**

> Normalize both sides through the same sanitizer the library read uses. For example, run stops through `parseStops(stops.map(serializeStop))`, and summary through `cleanText(summary, SUMMARY_MAX) ?? ''`, and title through `(title ?? '').trim()`. Alternatively, sanitize notes and dates at edit or save time, so that the working trip and the saved entry are byte-identical after Save.
